Flightline Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, or an account.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. It is never sent to us.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • Where the law requires it, you're asked for consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice later.
  • We don't sell data. We don't run any servers that receive your data.

Who we are

Flightline is a mobile game made by OpenCircuit Games ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Flightline app on Android and iOS. If you have a question, email opencircuitgames@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Flightline saves the following on your phone so the game remembers you:

  • Game progress: coins, unlocked aircraft and upgrades, missions, best distances and your logbook.
  • Settings such as sound and vibration.
  • Small counters used to decide when to show ads or a rating prompt, so you don't see them too often.

To pick the home airport on your boarding pass, the game reads your phone's time zone setting. It doesn't ask for location permission, and the time zone never leaves your phone.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Flightline has no sign-in and no chat. We don't use our own analytics or tracking tools, and we don't receive any personal data from the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Flightline shows ads through Google AdMob. Some ads are optional rewarded videos, for example to keep flying after a crash or to double your coins. Others are occasional full-screen ads between flights.

To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and send to Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking on iOS).
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (country or city).
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them.
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information about the ads SDK.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, personalize ads. Google acts under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. In regions that require it, such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Flightline shows Google's consent form before ads load. Where required, the game also offers a privacy options entry so you can change your choice at any time.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings under Google, then Ads, or Privacy, then Ads, depending on your device.
  • iOS. Ads are only personalized with your IDFA if you allow tracking when asked. You can change this in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • You can also manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Ratings and store links

Flightline may ask you to rate the game. On Android this uses Google Play's in-app review, and on iOS it uses Apple's rating prompt. Your rating and review go directly to Google or Apple under their policies. We don't see who left them beyond what the store shows publicly. Buttons that open the store page or this policy open your browser or store app.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell personal data, and we don't share any data with anyone because we don't receive any. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, through the AdMob SDK described above.

Children

Flightline is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent and think your child has used the game in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help.

Keeping and deleting data

Game data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google's own retention policies. You can reset your advertising ID at any time, which separates future ad data from past data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or California's CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or opt out of certain uses of your personal data. Because we don't hold any personal data about you, requests about ad data are best made to Google using the links above. If you need help, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your phone's own security. Data sent by the ads SDK travels to Google over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Flightline handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
